# Sponsor Onboarding Flow — Design

Fleet design (workflow `wf_35466b53-daf`, 41 agents): 5 brainstorm loops + 3 solution loops, grounded in existing sponsor code + the invariant constitution. Scope: enter -> establish business -> upload poster/banner/vertical/square upfront -> assign event-type category (expandable to individual types). **Payment out of scope.** Confidence: high.

## Recommended flow: Prepared-to-Go-Live Sponsor Onboarding (Invite-Anchored, Progressive-with-Guardrails, hardened)

Built on the highest-scoring spine (Progressive-with-Guardrails, total 52-53) with the best grafts from the other candidates (confirm-four-from-one asset grid; pre-checked smart-default bundle; uniform-slot proof card; honest reach preview), then hardened against every fixable hole from the loop-3 adversarial probes. Two structural decisions make it buildable against the EXISTING model: (1) the ORGANIZER-MINTED INVITE is the canonical v1 entry, so brand_id/event_id come from the existing admin/brand-scoped model and NO new self-serve auth surface is needed to ship (#9 satisfied; pure cold self-serve deferred to Phase-2 behind the same draft machinery); (2) all draft state lives in NEW draft tables (sponsor_business_draft + sponsor_asset_draft + a category_selections JSON on the draft) that are STRUCTURALLY unreachable by the resolver, so a partial sponsor can never consume share of voice â€” rather than relying on a status flag the resolver would have to be taught to honor. Order is identity->frame->furnish->categorize (NOT category-first): the business-type is captured at step 2 so the category picker can lead with a single-tap smart default, capturing Category-First's honest pricing-seam framing without its cold-open friction. Payment is OUT OF SCOPE; the flow stops at a Review compliance receipt that names FREQUENCY as the only future paid lever. Every hole rated blocker/major in loop 3 is resolved inline in the steps below (server-side geocoding for is_local; brand-scope on the event-types/areas writes; real four-format asset columns + a server COALESCE placeholder; a real template<->registry mapping table; raster-only crop source with server re-validation; per-format focal points; persisted bundle-vs-custom intent; reach-preview decoupled from type count).

### Steps

**1. S0 - Entry: organizer invite (canonical v1); cold self-serve deferred to Phase-2**
- Action: Sponsor enters via an organizer-minted single-use expiring invite link. The invite token pre-binds brand_id + event_id + optional campaign_id and creates a status=draft sponsor_business_draft row keyed to a draft_token; the invite token IS the sponsor's scoped credential for the whole draft (no account, no magic-link round-trip). A skippable one-screen pre-flight card names what to have handy (logo image >=400px, business address, which event kinds) with an email/SMS 'resume later' link scoped to the draft token. Cold self-serve email entry is explicitly Phase-2 (no brand_id source) behind the same draft tables.
- Captures: invite_token (brand_id + event_id + optional campaign_id), draft_token, any organizer-prefilled business fields, resume contact. A sponsor_business_draft row is created; NOTHING is written to the live sponsors table.
- Invariant: #9: the organizer is already admin-auth + brand-scoped, so the invite is the only entry that cleanly supplies brand_id under the existing model - no new tenancy surface needed to ship. #1: sponsor is the NAMED world; resume contact is never coupled to attendee identity. #25 integrity: draft state lives in sponsor_business_draft (NOT sponsors), so it is structurally invisible to resolveSponsorsForEvent/allocateShares - no partial sponsor can consume share.

**2. S1 - Establish business (one-screen confirm card, server-side geocode + scrape prefill)**
- Action: One vertical mobile card: name; website (paste triggers validateLogoUrl + fetchAndProxyLogo on a hard 4s budget with a 'skip and type it in' escape from t=0 to grab a logo candidate); and one ADDRESS field with 'use my location' + typeahead. CRITICAL FIX: the client sends the ADDRESS STRING, not raw lat/lon; the worker geocodes server-side and derives h3_res9/h3_res7 via maybeTag, then compares to the pre-bound event's h3_res7 to derive is_local. A READ-ONLY badge flips to 'Local - locals collectively hold >=30% of rotation frequency' or 'Regional' with a lock glyph + 'set by your location, not buyable' tooltip and NO toggle. Self-geocoded locals are flagged for organizer review before promotion and the raw address is logged for audit. Failed logo scrape shows an amber (not red) tile: paste-different-URL / upload / UEC-blue monogram fallback. Business type is captured via an explicit dropdown (more honest than a scrape classifier) to seed the step-5 smart default.
- Captures: sponsor_business_draft: name, website_url, logo candidate (nullable), address STRING -> server-geocoded lat/lon -> server-derived h3_res9/h3_res7, derived is_local (read-only, review-flagged if self-geocoded), business_type (explicit). Autosaved.
- Invariant: #25 is_local geo-derivation (LIVE): FIX for the loop-3 blocker - is_local is derived from a SERVER-geocoded address string, never from client-supplied lat/lon, so it cannot be spoofed into the corridor; self-declared locals are review-gated + audit-logged. No is_local toggle. #14: single column, 16px inputs, typeahead pushes not floats over the sticky CTA. #1: per-field 'why we ask'. Reuses sponsor-logo.ts validate/proxy (no R2, no new fetch stack).

**3. S2 - How placement works (non-interactive uniform-slot proof, swipe-past)**
- Action: A one-screen framing card between identity and assets: renders the EXACT uniform slot every sponsor gets across poster/display/public surfaces at IDENTICAL size, copy 'same size, same spots for everyone - you choose which events and how often, never a bigger slot.' Covenant numbers (floor 30 / caps 40-20 / weights 7-3-1) are server-provided from the sponsor-allocation mirror, never hardcoded in the template. Swipe-away, not a gate; pre-frames assets as surface COVERAGE, not purchasable prominence.
- Captures: None (framing). Optionally records covenant_acknowledged_at on the draft.
- Invariant: #25 sponsor_share_covenant (LIVE): teaches that SIZE/SLOT/POSITION are uniform and non-purchasable - matches placement_formats forced to UNIFORM_PLACEMENT_FORMATS and filterSponsorsForSurface returning ALL sponsors. #3 hygiene: covenant numbers + UEC pad color come from server values originating in the brain-mirrored modules; the onboarding template is added to the OSE-336 drift-check surface so a hardcoded 30/40/20 fails CI.

**4. S3 - Capture all four assets upfront (one-image auto-derive grid) [GUARDRAIL 1]**
- Action: A four-tile grid - POSTER / BANNER / VERTICAL / SQUARE. The sponsor drops ONE hi-res RASTER source (PNG/JPEG/WebP only for the derive source - SVG/animated GIF are rejected as crop sources with an explanatory message, though SVG may remain a pass-through logo) or reuses the scraped logo, and sets a PER-FORMAT focal point (not one global focal); the client canvas derives each crop at its true target aspect ratio and shows the actual crop (not just a green check) so cross-format conflicts are visible. Each tile has a per-format 'replace' override and a read-only 'renders on...' line. Oversized images are client-downscaled (no 413); EXIF honored; a source under 400px on the short edge is rejected server-side. The four derived/overridden outputs are RE-VALIDATED server-side (content-type + decoded dimensions + aspect) before a tile's green check is honored - the check reflects a server-validated asset, not merely that the client canvas ran. A no-image sponsor can seed a UEC-blue monogram, but see invariantNotes on reframing. GUARDRAIL: Go-Live is unreachable until all four format rows resolve server-side.
- Captures: sponsor_asset_draft rows: (draft_token, format, url, content_type, focal_x, focal_y, crop_rect, validated_at) per format, plus source image + sampled pad color; re-keyed to sponsor_id at promotion.
- Invariant: #25 HARD WALL: four formats are RENDER/COVERAGE inputs only, never purchasable placement. FIX (loop-3 major): to prevent 'which formats I provided' becoming a de-facto slot, every sponsor MUST render on every surface regardless of uploads - the serve route's COALESCE (override->derived->logo->UEC-monogram) must ALWAYS yield a non-empty asset; an invariant test asserts a sponsor with one format still appears on all four surfaces at identical frequency. #3: pad/monogram color from a brain-read UEC palette endpoint, never a hex literal. #14: single-column grid, sequential-crop degrade at 375px. NOTE: because the server COALESCE guarantees non-empty renders, the four-tile guardrail is reframed as 'give us your best image; we fit it to all four spots' - completeness is real (four validated rows) but a monogram-only sponsor is honestly labeled, not silently 'complete'.

**5. S4 - Category selection: bundle cards with pre-checked smart default [GUARDRAIL 2]**
- Action: Single-column category cards built by joining event_templates.category to event_type_registry THROUGH A NEW template_event_types mapping table (see dataModelChanges) via a NEW public read endpoint - so each card can honestly show UEC cluster code/name and per-template clearance derived from its mapped event types. The business_type dropdown (step 2) drives ONE pre-checked smart-default bundle at the top with a big Confirm (the ~80% single-tap path), sourced from a seeded business_type->category mapping table; low-confidence/unknown business_type means NO pre-check. A large left-side checkbox selects a whole bundle in one tap. A sticky bottom reach-preview shows THREE decoupled, price-free numbers, each labeled with which control drives it: matching upcoming corridor events (h3_res7 aggregate - responds to category/type toggles), projected SOV as a CONTENTION-DEPENDENT range (responds to tier + how many OTHER sponsors share the event, NOT to your type count; states 'with few sponsors a single sponsor can hold up to 100%; caps bind only as the corridor fills'), and a 'same size on every surface' reassurance. Empty-corridor state is honest with notify-me. Restricted/kid-adjacent clusters show a shield and cannot be bulk-selected. GUARDRAIL: no empty selection; the pre-checked bundle satisfies it in one tap. Every category's smart-default is tested to resolve to >=1 selectable template.
- Captures: category_selections JSON persisted on the draft: [{category, mode:'whole'|'custom', excluded:[template_ids], consent_flags}]; resolved to a template_ids union at save. Category recorded as the future SCOPE unit (not price unit).
- Invariant: #3: card UEC codes + clearance read live from event_type_registry via the mapping table + new public endpoint - never hardcoded. #25: category = WHICH events (scope), never size/slot/frequency; SOV preview reads the allocation mirror and is explicitly contention-dependent so it cannot overpromise a bounded share in the sparse regime. PRICING FIX: category is a SCOPE choice DECOUPLED from the price axis (frequency/tier) - the copy says 'declare coverage now; choose frequency band (price) later', never implying category maps to a price. #9: category edits write to the DRAFT here, NOT to the admin endpoint.

**6. S4b - Expand a category -> pick individual event types (inline accordion)**
- Action: Each card's 'fine-tune' chevron expands that category's event types INLINE as a big-left-checkbox list (no nested modal, no horizontal scroll). Whole-bundle is the baseline: every type starts checked; unchecking grows a live '5 of 7' diff chip with an amber 'edited' dot and a one-tap undo, and this bundle-vs-custom intent is PERSISTED in the draft's category_selections JSON (so it survives save/resume and later edits, since the flat sponsor_event_types join is lossy). Each row shows its uec_code mono chip + plain clearance badge read live via the mapping table. Restricted/kid-adjacent clusters CANNOT be bulk-selected: tapping forces the expanded list so each restricted type is consciously, individually checked (consent gate), and restricted types are excluded from the smart default. 'Select all in <category>' flips the header back to whole.
- Captures: Updated category_selections JSON (mode/excluded/consent_flags per category); the final template_ids union is recomputed from this structure at save.
- Invariant: #3: labels/uec_codes/clearance come live from event_type_registry via template_event_types - this is where the two registries are reconciled (now a real seeded mapping, not fiction). #25: toggling types changes only WHICH events matched, never size/slot/frequency. FIX: bundle-vs-custom is persisted in the draft JSON, not client-only, so the diff chip survives reload. Consent gate has real clearance data to gate on only because the mapping table exists.

**7. S5 - Review & Go-Live (compliance receipt) - STOP before payment**
- Action: Single scroll: (1) identity card with the read-only local badge; (2) a four-surface live mirror that renders the SAME serve endpoint production uses (not a client preview) at identical uniform slot size, so it cannot show green while production shows empty; (3) a per-surface coverage ledger every row green because the serve COALESCE always yields a non-empty asset; (4) selected categories as a receipt with per-category 'whole' or '5 of 7' state read from the persisted JSON; (5) covenant footer 'same size and spots as every sponsor; local is set by your location and can't be bought; locals collectively hold >=30% of rotation frequency, none above 40%.' Honest status pill names the single blocker if any. 'Go live' promotes the draft: creates the real sponsors row(s) for the pre-bound event (server FORCES uniform placement_formats and RE-DERIVES is_local server-side), populates the four asset columns, and fans out sponsor_event_types via a brand-scoped write. A greyed 'Next: choose how often you rotate (frequency)' teaser marks the payment seam without designing it. Flow STOPS.
- Captures: Promotion: live sponsors row (name, logo_url + poster/banner/vertical/square URLs, server-geo-derived is_local, server-forced uniform placement_formats, default tier = base share weight), sponsor_event_types fan-out (union from JSON), assets re-keyed draft->sponsor_id, covenant_acknowledged_at. status draft->active only when all four asset rows resolve.
- Invariant: #25 (LIVE): promotion forces placement_formats to UNIFORM_PLACEMENT_FORMATS and re-derives is_local server-side; the review UI never sends either. tier defaults to a share WEIGHT, not a price. Only AFTER promotion does the row enter buildShareRotation. The live mirror renders the real serve endpoint so it physically cannot lie. #1: privacy ledger ('reach is corridor-level, never person-level'). PAYMENT OUT OF SCOPE.

**8. (cross-cutting) Progress rail + autosave + resume**
- Action: Bottom thumb-zone 4-step rail (Business > Assets > Categories > Review) + a 'Saving / Saved / Offline' pill. Every field commits to the draft tables; 'save & finish later' texts/emails a resume URL scoped to the invite/draft token; cold reload lands on 'Welcome back - step 3 of 4' with bundle-vs-custom intent intact (from the persisted JSON).
- Captures: Continuous autosave to sponsor_business_draft / sponsor_asset_draft / category_selections JSON; resume link scoped to the invite/draft token.
- Invariant: #25 airtight guard: drafts live in separate tables structurally unreachable by resolveSponsorsForEvent/allocateShares - not a status flag the resolver must honor. #14: rail in thumb zone, CTA sticky above keyboard (visualViewport). #9: resume link authorized by the invite/draft token, not a new auth surface.

## Alternative

Runner-up: Category-First (Frame-Then-Furnish), where the event-type category picker is the FIRST substantive screen, before establishing the business. Prefer it ONLY when (a) the payment step already exists and pricing is genuinely category-anchored (so leading with the priced unit is honest rather than aspirational), AND (b) entry is predominantly cold self-serve with NO business-type signal available up front (no invite prefill, no scrape) - in that world there is no smart-default to earn from establishing identity first, so the ordering cost of category-first disappears and its 'set expectations before effort' benefit dominates. Given the current model (no price data; invite-anchored v1 that DOES yield a business_type at step 2), the recommended identity-first ordering strictly dominates because it enables the single-tap smart default and a personalized reach preview. A lighter fallback within the recommended flow: if the four-format asset store or the template_event_types mapping table cannot land in v1, ship an interim that captures only the single logo_url (today's model) plus the category bundle over sponsor_event_types, deferring the four-format grid and per-template clearance badges - the flow degrades gracefully to existing capability without violating any invariant."

## Data-model changes

- NEW table sponsor_business_draft(draft_token PK, brand_id, event_id, campaign_id NULL, name, website_url, logo_url NULL, address_raw, lat, lon, h3_res9, h3_res7, is_local_derived, is_local_review_flag, business_type, category_selections JSON, covenant_acknowledged_at, status DEFAULT 'draft', created_at, updated_at) - decoupled from the NOT-NULL sponsors.event_id; structurally invisible to the resolver so drafts never consume share of voice.
- NEW table sponsor_asset_draft(id, draft_token FK, format IN(poster,banner,vertical,square), url, content_type, focal_x, focal_y, crop_rect JSON, validated_at) - per-format focal point/crop, re-keyed to sponsor_id at promotion.
- ALTER sponsors ADD poster_url / banner_url / vertical_url / square_url (nullable) + per-format content_type + validated_at, reusing validateLogoUrl/fetchAndProxyLogo. Four formats become REAL persisted columns, not client-side illusion. (sponsors.logo_url remains the pass-through/monogram fallback source.)
- NEW table template_event_types(template_id FK event_templates.id, uec_code FK event_type_registry.uec_code) - the MISSING join key. Seeded deliberately. Without it, the category picker cannot show UEC chips or clearance badges and the consent gate has nothing to gate on. This is a BLOCKING migration, not 'client-side sugar'. Derive a per-template max legal_clearance_tier across mapped event types, stored queryable.
- NEW table business_type_category_map(business_type, category, is_default) - seeds the step-5 smart-default bundle from the step-2 business_type dropdown; tested so every category's default resolves to >=1 selectable template.
- NEW R2 key namespace sponsors/{sponsor_id or draft_token}/{format} authorized by the invite/draft token (current UPLOADS_BUCKET path is events/{eventId}/... and admin-only).
- MODIFY serve path: generalize GET /:id/logo to GET /:id/asset/:format with a server-side COALESCE override->derived->logo->generated-UEC-monogram-SVG that ALWAYS returns 200 image bytes (never the current bare 404/422/502). Add a test asserting 200 image bytes for a sponsor with all asset fields NULL.
- Persist bundle-vs-custom intent in sponsor_business_draft.category_selections JSON (mode/excluded/consent_flags per category) - the flat sponsor_event_types join is lossy and cannot reconstruct '5 of 7 (edited)' after fan-out.

## Gaps

- Cold self-serve entry (fully sponsor-initiated 'ENTER the platform') is deferred to Phase-2: it has no brand_id source under the current model, so v1 is invite-gated. The draft machinery is designed to accept a cold path later without rework.
- No price data exists anywhere on the sponsor path (tier is a share weight); 'adhere to pricing' has nothing concrete to adhere to yet. Category is captured as a SCOPE unit; the price axis (frequency/tier) is a separate, deferred concern.
- The @ocr/ui React/Tailwind surface (Phase 3) is 'not started' - the entire /sponsor/onboard shell, canvas crop, and live mirror cannot ship on today's server-rendered admin.html without new surface work.
- business_type classification: the honest v1 uses an explicit dropdown; an automated scrape-based classifier does not exist and should not be assumed.
- No keyboard/screen-reader-operable path is yet specified for the canvas focal-point crop and the 'tap forces expansion' consent gate (a11y gap flagged in loop 3 mobile judging).

## Risks

- If the draft-exclusion is implemented as a status flag on the sponsors table instead of separate draft tables, a default is_active=1 draft would leak into buildShareRotation and consume share of voice (#25 violation). Mitigation: keep drafts in separate tables; add a regression test asserting a draft never appears in buildShareRotation.
- If the serve-path COALESCE placeholder is not built, the Review live-mirror shows green while production renders broken-image boxes (the UI lies). Mitigation: implement the always-200 monogram serve BEFORE claiming coverage in the UI; render the real endpoint in the mirror.
- template_event_types is a non-trivial seeding effort (32 templates x 47 registry types, no natural key); a wrong mapping silently mislabels clearance and could let a restricted type be bulk-selected. Mitigation: treat as a reviewed, tested migration with per-template clearance derivation tests.
- Auto-derived crops from a single source can look poor for wide/text-heavy logos even with per-format focal points; a favicon-class source produces garbage. Mitigation: sub-400px rejection + per-format previews at true aspect + per-format override.
- Adding resolvePerIdSponsorScope to the event-types/areas writes is a behavior change to existing admin endpoints; verify no current admin workflow relied on cross-scope writes. Mitigation: audit callers, return NOT_FOUND for out-of-scope, add tests.
- Reach-preview SOV band can mislead in the sparse single-sponsor regime (allocateShares returns 100%). Mitigation: render it as a contention-dependent range, not a fixed 'you get X%'.

## Open questions for the (deferred) payment step

- How does a self-serve FREQUENCY choice become an authorized tier? tier is admin-set today and is a share WEIGHT, not a price - the payment step must define who authorizes a band and how it maps to the 6 tiers / SOV_TIER_MAP weights.
- What is the price per frequency band, and is it flat or modulated by SCOPE breadth (number of categories/templates matched, corridor size)? Category is scope, not price - payment must decide whether scope influences cost.
- Does buying frequency ever interact with the caps/floor (premium 40 / mid 20 / local 30)? Payment must confirm a purchase can never let a sponsor exceed a cap or displace the local floor - the covenant math must remain the ceiling.
- What is the price/refund behavior for the sparse regime where a single sponsor necessarily holds 100% share regardless of tier? A sponsor may pay for 'premium' and receive the same 100% as 'base' in an empty corridor.
- Where does checkout sit relative to Go-Live - does a sponsor go live (rotating) before paying, or is activation gated on payment? This determines whether promotion sets is_active immediately or a pending-payment status.
- What price data model is needed (a per-band price table; per-category or per-corridor modifiers) - none exists today, so payment must introduce the first price columns.
- Billing tenancy: is the sponsor billed directly, or via the organizer/brand (campaigns)? The invite-anchored model binds sponsors to a brand - payment must decide the payer and the #9-scoped billing entity.
