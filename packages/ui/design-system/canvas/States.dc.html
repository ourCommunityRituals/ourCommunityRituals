Interaction states · designing for the 50–75 reader

Make every mistake cheap

People in this age range don't use software less carefully. They use it with less confidence. So every error should be obviously recoverable in one step, and nothing should move unless the person asked it to.

Do the thing, then offer Undo for 10 seconds. Keep a real confirm only for things that can't be undone, like taking a live event down.
Check on leave, never mid-word That email is missing its ending. Try hello@maplefair.org. Errors say what happened, why and what to do. The error line's space is saved from the start, so nothing jumps.
Explain before, not after Why we ask: it sets your corridor, the few blocks around your venue. The hint sits under the label before anyone types. A hint that only shows up after a mistake feels like a telling-off.

Nothing expires while someone is reading. If a session must end, warn at 20 minutes with one tap to keep going.
Space, focus, distance
Targets are 48px with 12px between them and a 3px focus ring. Destructive actions sit 24px away, never beside the main action.
A glyph never travels alone No icon-only buttons, no hover-only hints, no swipe or long-press actions. Every action has a visible control with a word on it.
